    Gene 02 · Thresholds
    Gene 02 What we explore

    The thresholds we live on

    Liminal, from the Latin limen: threshold. We don't pick a side of old divides; we work in the space between, where new things grow.

    “The space where we are betwixt and between the familiar and the completely unknown. There alone is our old world left behind, while we are not yet sure of the new existence. That's a good space where genuine newness can begin.” — Richard Rohr

    Gene 05 · Practices
    Gene 05 What we do

    Practices: what is expected of you

    You are not a guest at a retreat. You are a co-creator of a temporary community, and you share responsibility for how it goes.

    Your goals · 1 of 3

    Personal

    Your own goal for the lunation

    Your goals · 2 of 3

    Local

    Something for the people & land here

    Your goals · 3 of 3

    Global

    A cause that matters for the planet

    Before you arrive

    Align

    • Read this page & the onboarding docs
    • Join ReGenerativa APS
    • Agree how you'll contribute (8 capitals)
    • Confirm dates before booking travel
    • Arrive with the new moon
    Every day

    Contribute

    • 2 h/day member · 4 h/day volunteer
    • Claim tasks via the bot, follow through
    • See a job? It's yours
    • Follow the protocols
    • No shoes inside · label your food
    Every lunation

    Co-create

    • Gift a talk, workshop or practice
    • Join circles & feedback
    • Voice tensions with proposals
    • Propose projects
    • Share meals, fire & collective cleaning
    Before you leave

    Close the cycle

    • 3 days' notice
    • Clean room, sheets & mat
    • Return what you borrowed
    • Deep clean = bonus + deposit back
    • Send a 2–5 min video
    Counts as contribution

    What counts

    • Maintenance
    • Cleaning
    • Cooking
    • Gardening
    • Childcare
    • Events & facilitation
    • Emotional support
    • Offering workshops
    • Approved projects
    Doesn't count

    What doesn't

    • Social activities
    • Attending circles
    • Attending workshops

    Unkept commitments have a cost: fairness keeps the commons alive.
