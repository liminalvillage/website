    Gene 03 · Lineage
    Gene 03 Where we come from

    A living constellation of guides

    Honor the wisdom of the past, question the assumptions of the present, and embody the possibilities of the future.

    Not a fixed pantheon, but ancestors, visionaries and guides from both spiritual and intellectual traditions. The intellectual guides help us understand the patterns, the spiritual guides help us embody them, and the village is the experiment where both meet: the place where the liminal becomes livable.

    No gurus. No unquestionable truths. Even our guides are there to be challenged. Wisdom belongs to the relationships and the living system, not to any single authority. Every guide brings strengths and blind spots: tap a name to meet them, and the question we put to them.

    Three movements

    Remember, reimagine, regenerate

    1. Rememberingour relationship with ourselves, each other and the living world: Watts, Kimmerer, Thich Nhat Hanh, indigenous wisdom traditions
    2. Reimaginingwhat is possible beyond today's institutions: McKenna, Fuller, Fresco, Illich
    3. Regeneratingturning that into real places, relationships and practices: Ostrom, Meadows, Holmgren, Schwartz, Alexander

    You are not a guest at a retreat. You are a co-creator of a temporary community, and you share responsibility for how it goes.

    • Local: something for the people & land here
    • Global: a cause that matters for the planet
    Before you arrive

    Align

    • Read this page & the onboarding docs
    • Join ReGenerativa APS
    • Agree how you'll contribute (8 capitals)
    • Confirm dates before booking travel
    • Arrive with the new moon
    Every day

    Contribute

    • 2 h/day member · 4 h/day volunteer
    • Claim tasks via the bot, follow through
    • See a job? It's yours
    • Follow the protocols

    • Community Facilitator: meetings, mediation, learning

    Gene 08 · Tools
    Gene 08 What we use

    Tools: a community operating system

    The village runs on Holons, open-source software (AGPL) that we build and use ourselves. It makes tasks, roles, time, resources and money visible to everyone, so the community can organise itself without a boss or a back office.

    Each community has its own space, starting from its Telegram group: there is nothing to install and no separate account to create.

    Holons · three ways in

    Chat, browser, wall screen

    • HolonsBot the everyday surface, right in the group chat
    • Dashboard the full web app: maps, forms, finances
    • Kiosk a touch screen for shared spaces: anyone can look, members log in with Telegram to edit
    Holons · the work

    Tasks, shifts & roles

    • Tasks & quests: create, join, log hours, mark done; repeating chores
    • Shifts: see the week's shifts, tap to take or drop one
    • Roles: who holds cooking, garden, space and the rest
    • Checklists & shopping list: shared lists, /buy milk
    Holons · rhythm

    Calendar & lunations

    • Agenda & events: what's coming up, and adding your own
    • Lunation prompts: a daily moon-phase suggestion for the group
    • Big Talk: questions to get to know each other beyond small talk
    Holons · sharing

    Money, things & supplies

    • Expenses & balances: log a shared cost, split it, settle up in the fewest payments
    • Offers & requests: what you can give, lend or sell, and what you need
    • Library of things: borrow and return tools, books, equipment
    • Pantry: bulk supplies tracked, with a buy list from restock targets
    Holons · together

    Deciding & recognising

    • Proposals & voting: raise a proposal, vote yes, no or abstain
    • Appreciation: thank someone publicly; contributions become visible
    • Values, needs & Ikigai: see what you share with others
    • AI help: summarise a long chat, get facilitation tips, ask the onboarding docs
    How we use it

    A lunation on Holons

    1. Arrivejoin the lunation's group; the bot walks you through your profile, skills and Ikigai
    2. Every daycheck the agenda, take a shift or a task, log hours and shared expenses
    3. When a tension shows upturn it into a proposal and let the circle vote
    4. As the moon closesappreciations, balances settled, the lunation reviewed together
