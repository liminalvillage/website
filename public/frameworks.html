Frameworks

The maps our guides left us. Each one comes from someone in our living constellation of guides, and they are arranged here along the same six dimensions: how we see, how we share, how systems behave, how we build, how we tend the land, and how we change from the inside. None of them is the territory; each is a lens that makes some things easier to see.

Honor the wisdom of the past, question the assumptions of the present, and embody the possibilities of the future.

No gurus, no unquestionable truths: every map has blind spots, and every guide is there to be challenged. Meet the guides →

The constellation keeps growing

These frameworks are tools, not doctrine. Our DNA describes how they show up in daily community life — the agreements we keep, the roles we hold and the tools we coordinate with. Is there a map you think belongs here?
