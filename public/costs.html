Costs that aren't in the figures above yet. Some could become their own reserve lines.

  • Hospitality-related laundry, guest linen turnover, and extra wear from short stays or events.
  • Fire safety equipment, extinguishers, alarms, first-aid supplies, and periodic checks.
  • Drains, pumps, gutters, and stormwater maintenance beyond the septic reserve.
  • Tree pruning, hedge cutting, pest control, and seasonal gardening peaks beyond the monthly reserve.
  • Legal and compliance costs for contracts, safety, hospitality rules, and occasional professional advice.
  • Contingency reserve for storm damage, water leaks, fallen trees, urgent appliance failure, or unexpected structural repairs.
Prepared by Laura on 12 March 2026, with help from ChatGPT, to give more clarity on the yearly and monthly running costs of Liminal Village. The work started when the NAO collective, gathering at Liminal Village, asked about running costs to help shape agreements between Liminal Village and the NAO. The maintenance and reinvestment reserves are AI-generated estimates and are meant to be refined over time.
