01 · Why we exist

Purpose

Liminal Village exists to prototype a regenerative civilization, weaving together community living, healing practices and open-source technologies, and sharing everything we learn.
Extraction → Regeneration
Isolation → Belonging
Scarcity → Shared abundance
The problem

Our answer

A real-world lab you can live in

Vision

A network of regenerative communities

Mission

Upgrade the operating system of society

02 · What we explore

The thresholds we live on

Liminal, from the Latin limen: threshold. We don't pick a side of old divides; we work in the space between, where new things grow.

“The space where we are betwixt and between the familiar and the completely unknown. There alone is our old world left behind, while we are not yet sure of the new existence. That's a good space where genuine newness can begin.” — Richard Rohr

05 · What we do

Practices: what is expected of you

You are not a guest at a retreat. You are a co-creator of a temporary community, and you share responsibility for how it goes.

Personal

Your own goal for the lunation

Local

Something for the people & land here

Global

A cause that matters for the planet

Before you arrive

Align

  • Read this page & the onboarding docs
  • Join ReGenerativa APS
  • Agree how you'll contribute (8 capitals)
  • Confirm dates before booking travel
  • Arrive with the new moon
Every day

Contribute

  • 2 h/day member · 4 h/day volunteer
  • Claim tasks via the bot, follow through
  • See a job? It's yours
  • Follow the protocols
  • No shoes inside · label your food
Every lunation

Co-create

  • Gift a talk, workshop or practice
  • Join circles & feedback
  • Voice tensions with proposals
  • Propose projects
  • Share meals, fire & collective cleaning
Before you leave

Close the cycle

  • 3 days' notice
  • Clean room, sheets & mat
  • Return what you borrowed
  • Deep clean = bonus + deposit back
  • Send a 2–5 min video

Counts as contribution

  • Maintenance
  • Cleaning
  • Cooking
  • Gardening
  • Childcare
  • Events & facilitation
  • Emotional support
  • Offering workshops
  • Approved projects

Doesn't count

  • Social activities
  • Attending circles
  • Attending workshops
Unkept commitments have a cost: fairness keeps the commons alive.
06 · How we organise

Roles & membership

Soft Holacracy: authority sits in roles, not people. Anyone can hold several roles, and roles change as the village does.

Village roles

Culinary

Meals, pantry, low food waste

Sustainability

Garden, produce, resources

Space & Logistics

Common spaces, supplies

Community Facilitator

Meetings, mediation, learning

Wellness

Healing, culture, support

Technology & Finance

Platforms, open finances

Member

2h / day

+ lunation or phase ticket, insurance included

Volunteer

4h / day

lower financial contribution

Sponsor

€

financial support; time welcome

donation · short stays only
